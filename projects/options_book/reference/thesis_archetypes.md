# Thesis-Archetypes (aus Strategy Artifact)

> Klassifikation für Screen + Vehicle-Wahl. Book-Ziel: **3–5 Namen** @ **$10K NAV**.

---

## Archetypes

### 1. Value Mean Reversion

- **These:** Crash on narrative, fundamentals intact
- **Vehicle:** LEAP Calls (8–12 Mon.)
- **Book-Anteil:** ~30–40 % NAV (bei 3–5 Namen: oft **1 Slot**)
- **Signale:** P/E/P/S compressed, institutional buying, revenue still growing, catalyst ahead

### 2. Momentum Re-rating

- **These:** Market reclassifies (old sector → new, e.g. telecom → AI infra)
- **Vehicle:** LEAPs (+ ggf. kurze Calls um Events)
- **Book-Anteil:** ~20–30 % NAV (**1 Slot**)
- **Signale:** P/S gap vs new peer set, strategic partners, revenue mix shift

### 3. Catalyst Binary

- **These:** Dated event, asymmetric setup
- **Vehicle:** Short Calls (2–5 Mon.) — **klein** @ $10K
- **Book-Anteil:** ~5–10 % NAV pro Trade max
- **Signale:** Implied move vs expected, IV rank, confirmed date; post-earnings entry often better

### 4. Policy / Structural Floor

- **These:** Govt/policy limits downside; options IV often too high
- **Vehicle:** **Common Shares**
- **Book-Anteil:** ~10–20 % NAV (**1 Slot** — z. B. MP)
- **Signale:** Structural backstop, high IV on low beta, slow catalyst ok

### 5. Cash / Optionality

- **These:** Deploy after IV crush or panic
- **Vehicle:** Cash — kein Trade
- **Book-Anteil:** **20–30 %** @ $10K empfohlen ($2–3K dry powder)

---

## Vehicle Decision Tree (aus Artifact)

```text
Liquid options chain?
  NO  → Shares (or skip)
  YES → IV < ~65% (fair)?
          NO  → Shares OR wait post-earnings IV crush
          YES → Catalyst within expiry?
                  YES → Match expiry to catalyst
                  NO  → LEAP (8–12m)
```
